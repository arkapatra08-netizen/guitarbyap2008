WHAT IS A GUITAR?

A guitar is a wooden musical instrument with six strings
that you play by pulling or strumming the strings with your fingers or a pick.

Read more

Main Types of Guitars

Acoustic: Has a hollow body to make the sound loud.
Electric: Uses wires and a speaker box to make the sound loud.
Bass: Has a longer neck and fewer or deeper strings for low notes.
