MY PERSONAL EXPERIENCE

GUITAR has always inspired me. Since my childhood I have always been attached to music; it was more due to my parents for their love towards music. And I have always listened to music of all genre, until one day I saw one of my school teachers to play few of my favourite songs at that time in guitar and naturally I was attracted to it and that was the beginning of my guitar journey way back in 2017 and now its 2026.

WHY DO I NEED A GUITAR?

What I personally think is that guitar or any form of music is needed by all of us. It serves as a medium of refreshment and also as a medium of conveying inner thoughts of mind. I believe everybody needs a form of music in their life to free them of their boredom and any other anxiety or depression issues.
